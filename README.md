Olist E-commerce Performance & Customer Insights

Project Overview
This project uses Power BI to analyse the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset. It investigates product sales, order fulfilment, customer satisfaction, and repeat purchasing.
The goal is to transform transactional data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations.

Business Questions
Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
What percentage of orders are delivered?
What proportion of customer reviews receive five stars?
What percentage of customers place repeat orders?
What actions could improve sales and customer retention?

Tools Used
Power BI
Power Query
DAX

Key Findings
Leading product category - Health & Beauty
Delivery rate - 97.02%
Five-star review rate - 57.78%
Repeat-customer rate - Approximately 3.01%

These results are preliminary and require validation before being treated as final business findings.

Business Recommendations
Investigate sales trends in leading product categories.
Explore loyalty programmes and targeted offers to encourage repeat purchases.
Analyse lower customer ratings to identify recurring problems.
Investigate cancelled and undelivered orders.
Monitor delivery performance and customer satisfaction.

Data and Methodology
The project uses order, customer, order-item, payment, review, product, seller, and product-category translation tables.
Power Query was used for data preparation, while DAX measures were created to calculate key performance indicators.
The current revenue measure sums product item prices and excludes freight charges. Delivery rate is not the same as on-time delivery rate, and the repeat-customer calculation must be validated against the data model.
